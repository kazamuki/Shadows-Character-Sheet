## Priority issues

- **[P0] The seat copy dims the readouts the GM opens it to read.**
  - `.ro-sheet button:disabled{opacity:.55; border-style:dashed}` (shadows.css:1655) catches the vitals tiles (`.cond.act`), the vitals pills (`.vpill.act`, on every tab) and Progression's 31 toggles, because the sheet draws them as buttons.
  - The tile labels and meta text drop from 5.65:1 to 2.62–2.72:1. HP "18" is 3.24:1 and Sanity "50" is 3.02:1.
  - Fix: dim and dash only the controls that act (`.btn`, the Condition palette), and leave the readers at full strength with no hover. The crew button's disabled look (dashed, no fade) is the better pattern. (harden)
- **[P1] The heading scale went flat, and the section voice is off-system.**
  - `.sec-h`, `.ref-sub` and `.next-h` are all `--mono` .74rem dim; a field label is .7rem dim mono. Only a rule separates a section from a sub-section from a field.
  - DESIGN.md:245 says section rules read in Aether Pulse, as the sheet's `.sect` does (`--violet-text`).
  - Fix: `.sec-h` in `--violet-text`. Sub-sections as the sheet's `.sub-sect` (Inter 600 .8rem), or keep Mono Caps but in `--text`. (typeset)
- **[P1] Play-first covers Encounters only.**
  - Each tab opens on an Add form with focus in it.
  - The cast and place pages are forms first, and their stat block is far down.
  - Fix: U3's dossier. (distill, shape)
- **[P2] Display-face titles on asks.** `.modal-head h2` is `--display` (pre-existing). Assessment A saw "?" render like "P" in "Delete this note?"; this was not confirmed glyph by glyph. (typeset)
- **[P2] Leftover copy.**
  - "Slot in a pack" (the empty Threats button, the site line).
  - "Delete this note?" doesn't name the note.
  - "Undo: The kitchen fire: 0 of 4" undoes to 1 of 4, so the line can read backwards. (clarify)
