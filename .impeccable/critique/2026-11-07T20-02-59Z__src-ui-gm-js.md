## Overall Impression

The keys work and stay out of the way. The weak spots sit around them: what Undo says and where focus goes after it, what N does at a reset, and the keys being invisible until you open ⋮. The biggest opportunity is making the reset step one key and one button.

## What's Working

- **N during a fight.** The NOW border moves, focus stays on Next, and the live region says who acts. That's the "players waiting" moment done right.
- **A principled key layer.** It's never in a field, select, IME, box or ⋮ menu, and one switch turns it off. `aria-keyshortcuts` comes and goes with the switch, and the open tab is `aria-current`.
- **Read first, Edit a press away** (U3) holds, and a digit from a member's page lands back on the list as a tab press does.

## Priority Issues

- **[P1] Undo's toast and Activity can't say what an encounter step was.**
  - *Why:* every Next is logged as "Changed <encounter>", so "Undone: Changed Achterberg's clinic, moonrise" doesn't tell the GM what came back.
  - *Fix:* label each encounter step ("Next turn: Juno Park", "Round 2's reset").
  - *Suggested command:* /impeccable clarify
- **[P1] The reset on a phone.**
  - *Why:* End the encounter is the bar's only button while Finish the round sits below the fold, and N does nothing there.
  - *Fix:* put Finish the round where Next was while the reset asks, and let N press it.
  - *Suggested command:* /impeccable layout
- **[P2] Focus after an undo lands on the first clock's −.**
  - *Why:* the steppers' `data-clkminus`/`data-clkplus` carry no value, so keepPlace's twin is the first one on the page. The next Enter changes the wrong clock. The toast's Undo does it too.
  - *Fix:* key the steppers by their clock.
  - *Suggested command:* /impeccable harden
- **[P2] The keys can't be recognised.**
  - *Why:* no digit shows on a tab, and the list doesn't map 1–8 to names.
  - *Fix:* name them in the Keys list ("1 Sessions · 2 Players · …").
  - *Suggested command:* /impeccable clarify
- **[P2] The table's tab row is named "Character sheet sections"** to a screen reader (`index.html:16`, never relabelled on a table).
  - *Suggested command:* /impeccable audit

## Persona Red Flags

**Alex (power user):**
- No Redo, and no key for Finish the round, Edit or Done.
- The digit-to-section map has to be memorised.
- Activity's identical rows make choosing what to undo blind.

**Sam (screen reader, keyboard):**
- The nav landmark says "Character sheet sections".
- Ctrl+Z with focus on the toast's own Undo, or on Finish the round at a reset, drops focus to the page body (checked in the preview).
- `aria-current` and `aria-keyshortcuts` read correctly.

**GM at the table:**
- On a phone the fight is the 6th of 8 tabs.
- The running fight's card looks like the planned ones.
- Ctrl/⌘+Z means nothing on a phone, where undo is ⋮ → Activity or the toast.

## Minor Observations

- The Keys box is titled Keys and its switch is labelled Keys.
- "Ctrl/⌘" shows on every platform.
- Activity opens with focus on its primary Undo, so A then Enter is an undo.
- The bar's chips truncate at 1024 on a dossier beside a long "Add to encounter: …".
- The dossier's prose runs about 100 characters a line at 1024.

## Questions to Consider

- Should undo be scoped to what's on screen, given a GM means "what I just did here"?
- Should Next *become* Finish the round, so one key and one button carry the whole round?
- Should a running fight pin itself as the first thing a table shows?
