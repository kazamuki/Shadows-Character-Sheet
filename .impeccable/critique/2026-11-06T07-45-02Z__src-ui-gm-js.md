## Design specificity verdict

The row is authored for Shadows: Oxanium HP over the sheet's own Health Level segments (`hlCellsOf`), the Pain Level tag, PROT/RES/INT armor lines, "Waiting: Achterberg finishes the sedative". The page around the rows is still a record editor: a Name field where the title should be, a mode toggle, rows, Add, Clocks, the plan, Delete. Nothing above the rows shows the fight's shape: who acts next, who is down, who is winning.

**Deterministic scan.** The CLI found 0 in gm.js, which it can't parse (template strings). In index.html it found 3, all chrome: pulsing-dot, dark-glow (brand cyan) and repeating-stripes. These are known false positives.

**Browser overlay**, run with the tab fronted, so the geometry is trustworthy:
- **#main:** 0 findings in every view at 1024px.
- **#main at 375px:** `body-text-viewport-edge` on `p.step-note` "From the Codex…" and the plan's prompt list (a 16px gutter; borderline false positive). `text-occlusion` on Take and Add a Condition is false: they sit inside a closed More `<details>`.
- **Chrome:** 3–4 per view. These are the brand violet, Inter, the hidden ⋮ menu and a page-wide `flat-type-hierarchy` (h3 15.2px against 15px body).
- **375px measurements:** no sideways overflow (scrollWidth 375 = clientWidth). `.enc-bar` is 113px on two lines, every button 44px; header plus bar is 207 of 812px.

## What's working

1. The HP readout over its segments is a real signature component, readable at a glance and on-brand. The health sentence underneath is unchanged as text, so screen readers and the tests read the same words.
2. The Hit panel is properly built. Apply and Cancel sit above one polite live region that updates in place. Enter applies. The over-2× ask lands on Cancel and returns to the field with its value.
3. Focus management is consistent: every action says where focus lands. Next keeps focus and announces through a region outside `main`.

## Priority issues

- **[P1] On a phone, the actor lands under the bar.** The bar now wraps to 113px. `.enc-row`'s `scroll-margin-top` is still `--hdr-off + 72px`, so Next scrolls the actor's name to y≈177, under the bar, which ends at y=207. Fix: give the row a scroll margin that clears the wrapped bar at ≤640px. (adapt)
- **[P1] The actor isn't on screen when the encounter opens.** Whose turn it is should be the first thing a GM sees. Fix: scroll to the active row on open, or carry the turn in the bar (U2's strip). (clarify, layout)
- **[P1] The phone Hit panel buries its outcome.** At 375px the damage field is at y≈582, Apply at ≈1055 and the preview at ≈1109, so with the keyboard up the GM types blind. Fix: on narrow widths, put damage, preview and Apply first, fold Type/Kind/AP/Where/From into a summary line, and show the result as well as the delta ("HP 25 → 16"). (adapt, distill)
- **[P2] Edit fields at play time.** A running fight still renders a Name input as its title and a Combat Sense input on every row, and it has no h1. Fix: while running, show the title as a heading with Rename, and Combat Sense as text. (distill)
- **[P2] More mixes three jobs.** Play (Take, Add a Condition, Delay), setup (Name, Goes last, Out, Side) and destruction (Remove). Group them; consider Add a Condition on the row. The click-to-set-turn on `.enc-head` is mouse-only and undiscoverable. (clarify, harden)

## Persona red flags

- **Alex (keyboard power user):** no key for Next (U5), no Esc on the Hit panel, no jump to Now. The row-head turn-set is mouse-only.
- **Sam (screen reader):** the running page has no h1, and its title is an input labelled "Name". Rows have no headings to jump between. Hit/Heal/More carry no row name. Good: aria-current on the actor, "X acts" announced, and the preview announced in place.
- **Casey (phone):** header plus bar take 25% of the screen. The actor scrolls under the bar. The Hit outcome is off screen. Next and End sit side by side at the same size (End only opens the wrap-up, so it is recoverable).
- **A GM mid-fight:** finding the actor takes a scroll on open, the resulting HP is mental arithmetic, and Add a Condition is a press into More.

## Minor observations

- The Now chip has the tip tag's dotted underline and help cursor (`.tag`, shadows.css:347). It should be `.tag.plain` with its own colour.
- Type defaults to Blade, an arbitrary first entry.
- On the light theme the HP number folds to the frame navy while the segments read violet, so health and harm share a family; the word **Down** carries the state.
- An ended encounter's seat rows show the seat's live HP, so a past fight reads today's numbers.
