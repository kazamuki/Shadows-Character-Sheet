## What's Working

- **The dossier's voice.** A display-face name, Mono Caps labels over Inter prose, and the violet-rule quote follow DESIGN.md exactly, and the note body stays inside its measure.
- **Focus and semantics.** Edit moves to the first field and Done back to Edit. Each aria-label names its record ("Edit Lt. Caz Ibarra", "Take one off Ibarra's patience"). Notes and threads carry aria-expanded, and a tab press stays on its tab.
- **Clocks where they're used.** Ibarra's patience steps in place on his dossier, on the faction and on cards, with the same words everywhere.

## Priority Issues

- **[P1] The stat block is buried and off-voice.** Below 1280px it comes last: at 1024px, 1367px down a 2083px page; on a phone, about 2.6 screens down, after a form. It reads as grey mono.
  - **Fix:** put it after Clocks below 1280px (the review's should-fix). Later, give `readBlockHtml` Oxanium figures and signed-modifier colour (a lead after U5).
  - **Suggested command:** /impeccable layout, then /impeccable typeset.
- **[P1] Read views still hold forms.** *Between them and the crew*'s add row (a select, 8 toggles, What, When, Where, Add) is open on every member's dossier (Decision 179, unchanged by order). The open session's read view is nearly its form (UQ31).
  - **Fix:** fold the line-add behind an "Add a line" button below the list. Decide whether an open session is a record or the night's console.
  - **Suggested command:** /impeccable distill.
- **[P2] The read view and Edit speak two vocabularies** (H4 above).
  - **Fix:** one label set and one order, and Edit beside the title everywhere, notes included.
  - **Suggested command:** /impeccable clarify.
- **[P2] On a phone the first screen of a list is chrome.** On Cast at 375, the table's title block, the quick-add and three filter rows push the first card to about 850px.
  - **Fix:** drop the title block on section tabs, and fold the filters.
  - **Suggested command:** /impeccable adapt.
