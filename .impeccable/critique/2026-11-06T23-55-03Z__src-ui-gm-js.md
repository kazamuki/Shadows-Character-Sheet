## Overall Impression

U2 makes GM mode play-first where it counts: the night is in one line on every tab, and each item lands you exactly where it says, with focus. The strip is careful work: it's a region, its items carry accessible names, hidden items are `display:none`, and it repaints on `update(false)`.

The biggest opportunity is that the strip's honesty stops at its edges:
- It goes away with the session at Write (taking Unexported with it when no fight runs).
- It gives no colour to urgency.
- On a phone it hides the one thing that's harm, Down.

## What's Working

1. **The Tonight strip's engineering.**
   - It's a persistent region node, so focus survives repaints.
   - It's painted from `update()`, so a clock's + and typing in Hours change it at once.
   - It has descriptive accessible names, and phone-hidden items are `display:none`.
   - The bar stays 53px, within the 20% budget at 390.
2. **Landing focus.**
   - A table opens at scrollY 0 on its h1, whatever Home's scroll was.
   - The fight jump focuses Next, the clock jump focuses +, and Hit focuses Damage.
   - Close-out's end focuses its h1, and Done returns to the Close-out head.
3. **The running encounter's head.**
   - The title is an h1 with a small Rename.
   - "CS 13" is a Mono Caps key plus a value.
   - More is three real groups (Play, Setup, Remove), with Remove ruled off.
