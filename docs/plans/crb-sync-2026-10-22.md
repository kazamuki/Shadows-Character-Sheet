# Plan — CRB sync: the sheet catches up to the 2026-10-22 mirror

**Status:** proposed, Ken's. Nothing built. Every question answered by Ken on 2026-10-22: the book's power
wording is the rule (a new pass, Y4), and YQ2–YQ4 on their defaults.
**Compiled:** 2026-10-22, against the mirror at `private/` `5541c61` (Ken's refresh) plus `a8d53ab` (the
eight `APPROVED - …` documents, mirrored the same day), game data 0.44 and app 0.52.0.
**Scope, on purpose:** keep the sheet and the Codex pack true to the book, and nothing more. It is sized to
finish and archive without moving `sheet-wishlist.md` or `gm-mode.md`: no pass here edits a session of
theirs, and none waits on one. **Cybernetics is not in this plan.** It is a system of its own, waiting on
design, and is **W90**; the low-SAN bands are **W134**.
**The other direction:** `crb-catch-up.md` is what the book needs from the sheet. This plan is what the
sheet needs from the book.
**Ids:** `YQ`_n_ are this plan's questions (§4). Y0, Y1, Y3 and Y4 are its passes (§2); Y2 was folded into Y0.

**How this was checked.** Every file the refresh changed was diffed against its previous version. The
split Threat Codex (`2610`–`2617`) was diffed against the frozen `Threat_Codex_Intro_Human` with list
numbering and HTML stripped, and the Codex pack was rebuilt from both by `gm/build-codex-pack.mjs`.
`npm run test:fast` was run against the new mirror. The `APPROVED` documents were each compared with the
chapter that carries them.
