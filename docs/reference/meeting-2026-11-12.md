## Natural attacks

This follows `meeting-2026-10-23.md`, which made natural attacks two a round by
default and left five details for Deighton. **Deighton's table report**
(0:31:59): Brogan's Werewolf with two attacks "felt correct to me."

**What was ruled:**

- **Natural weapons are part of you, not held** (Scott 0:49:03, Deighton
  0:49:35): claws, teeth, bare fists, and cyberware installed on the hands
  (jolt fist, knuckle studs, punch spike, tri-claws). They can't be disarmed
  (Scott 0:49:48).
- **Martial Arts applies to them** (Scott 0:47:16, Deighton 0:47:47 "Yes.
  Absolutely", 0:48:54 "Hundred percent"). A character trained in Martial Arts
  can make a Martial Arts attack with a natural weapon: bare fists, and claws.
- **Both attacks are the one Standard Action** (Deighton 0:50:55–0:51:22): "one
  of the natural attacks accounts for the attack action anyway … we're just
  [adding] a secondary attack since you're using this specific thing." This
  answers the 10-23 list's question 2.
- **Mastery adds one attack a rank, not another pair** (Scott 0:50:12, Deighton
  0:51:22, Scott 0:52:05 "If I have rank one, I get one additional swing. If I
  have rank two, I get two"). Two natural attacks, three with a rank of the
  mastery, four with two. This replaces the 10-23 note that "it stops at two"
  for any character who can hold the mastery. The mastery Advantages are
  Mortal-only, so for a Vampire or Werewolf it still stops at two.
- **Each hand uses its own weapon** (Scott 0:49:48): a punch spike on one hand
  gives its bonus to that hand's attacks only.
- **Hand cyberware stacks on one hand, and adds to BOD once** (Deighton
  0:54:23–0:55:26): BOD 8 with tri-claws (+8) and knuckle studs (+3) does 19,
  "not BOD plus, BOD plus." Mod slots will limit it (Scott 0:54:47, later).

**Read from what was said, not asked outright** (each needs Ken's yes before
the sheet relies on it, `plans/loadout-and-gear/L4-natural-attacks.md` §4):

- **Two attacks are two rolls** (the 10-23 list's question 1). Scott: "an
  average human succeeding on two claw attacks … armor would reduce it twice"
  (0:33:57); Deighton: "an armored target would just be shredded" (0:34:38).
- **A bare fist does BOD damage.** The book prints no unarmed damage. Deighton's
  model for natural weapons is "your BOD in straight physical damage" (0:35:57),
  and his cyberware arithmetic adds the studs' +3 to BOD alone (0:55:05).

**Still open** (never asked):

- Can a Martial Arts Combat Maneuver (Stun, Disarm) be one of the two (the 10-23
  list's question 3)? Martial Arts applies, and the book's maneuvers are
  unarmed Martial Arts attacks, but nobody said so.
- Can a natural attack share the round with a carried weapon, a knife and a bite
  (question 4)?
- Do two natural attacks need two free limbs (question 5)? Deighton counted
  "two fists, two legs" as what a combination draws on (0:50:55), which
  suggests not, but it wasn't the question.
- Does another character's Death Touch pierce Natural Armor (W107)?
- Do worn melee bonuses (`weaponBonus`, Decision 248) reach natural attacks?

**Answered 2026-11-15** (Scott, relayed by Ken; Deighton couldn't answer that
day). These close the five above for the sheet, as playtest rulings:

1. **A Combat Maneuver can be one of the two.**
2. **A natural attack never shares the round with an attack from a carried
   weapon.** No knife and a bite.
3. **The two need two different natural weapons:** two claws, or a claw and a
   bite, which works with something in the off hand. **Legs don't count.**
4. **Worn bonuses to melee weapons reach natural weapons** (Ken: "I would
   categorize them as melee-ranged weapons"). Scott noted that no item gives
   such a bonus yet, which is true: the only worn `weaponBonus` in the data is
   the SMARTLink Gloves', by feature.
5. **Natural Armor is immune to AP** (Scott: "It is your skin. that is why it is
   so potent and also relatively small"). So another character's Death Touch
   doesn't pierce it. The engine already does this for every AP but a spell's
   (Decision 219, Deighton and Ken, 2026-10-15), and Scott's answer doesn't
   reach the spell case.

**Opened by them** (for Scott, not blocking): with no legs, a mortal has two
fists. Is Melee Master's third attack a fist again, or does it need a third
natural weapon (`L4-natural-attacks.md`, LQ42)? **Ken, 2026-11-15:** another
fist.

## Spending SFR is a free action

(Ken 0:41:36, Deighton 0:42:34–0:43:24.) Spending SFR to engage a utility or
support power (AP, Preternatural Speed) is a free action, up to Rate of Use. An
attack made with a power is the Standard Action. Ken noted GM mode's references
say "using SFR is a standard action", and that it's to change. `0530`'s *Use a
Power* ("Using a Power is a Standard Action, unless otherwise specified") needs
the same distinction (`plans/crb-catch-up.md`).

## Luck

- **A boosted die never explodes** (0:21–0:25): a boost raises one die face by
  one, and more Luck can go on the same die. A boosted 10 is just a 10;
  explosions have to be natural. A boosted 1 is no longer a botch.
- **Luck on a breaker check** (1:27–1:35), which is percentile and roll-under:
  a boost is **+10 to the target**, up to its maximum (Sanity's 95). An explode
  is **a re-roll, keeping the better**. No crits on breaker checks.

## Other rulings with a sheet reader

- **An Essence check's critical success** is exceeding the TH by 3 or more
  (0:16). Spell overflow still counts every hit past the TH.
- **"EM pulse"** (EM beam, and so on) replaces "EMP" for electromagnetic
  effects. EMP means Empathy (0:25).
- **Integrity's abbreviation:** Durability (DUR) is the leading candidate, not
  adopted (0:26).
