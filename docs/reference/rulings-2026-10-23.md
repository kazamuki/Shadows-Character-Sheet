# Vehicle and Powered Armor rulings, 2026-10-23

Deighton's answers on vehicles and Powered Armor, for W91 (P14's vehicle
catalog), W69 (worn gear modifiers) and the vehicle-damage order after them.
He answered on two claude.ai artifact pages, which aren't part of the repo, so
his words are kept here exactly:

- **Round one:** *Vehicle and Powered Armor Rulings*. 31 questions, all answered
  2026-10-23. The SH questions are P14's PQ1–PQ8, each offering the order's
  default as *Recommended*.
- **Round two:** *Vehicle Rulings: Follow-ups*. 28 questions on the round-one
  answers he marked unsure, wanted to talk over, or noted beyond his pick.
  **11 answered by 2026-10-23; the other 17 are still with him.** Question ids
  are the round-one id plus a letter.

The pages showed him as a guest. He was the only person who answered either one.
**Nothing here is built yet.** What it does to P14 is in the order's Status.
Rule changes for the book (PA1's sentence, VO4's "Wheeler", PA9's prices,
dropping PROT, RES and mod slots from vehicles) go on `plans/crb-catch-up.md`
once the second round is settled.

## What is settled, and what isn't

- **P14's first cut.** SH1, SH2, SH4, SH5, SH6, SH7 and SH8 took the order's
  defaults (PQ1, PQ2, PQ4, PQ6, PQ7, PQ8, PQ5). SH3 (PQ3) picked the default
  but said he didn't understand it; it's reworded in round two and **open**.
- **Vehicles lose PROT and RES** (VD1, round two). A hit comes straight off
  Integrity. Deighton: "I want to keep this change for now, but I want to
  discuss it in person at some point." **Suits keep PROT and RES** (PA4,
  round two), taking a hit as PA2 says.
- **Suit prices go up 300%** (PA9a–d): Glimmer Man 20,020Ç, SS3 17,800Ç, Hyper
  Tactical 18,300Ç, Renzo X1 13,020Ç+. The Power Sleeves stay at 1,875Ç.
- **Under a suit:** armor that fits under street clothes (PA3; he picked the
  Concealable pieces).
- **Changing a suit's enhancements:** software swaps freely. Hardware takes
  Engineering (Robotics in niche cases) and the GM's time: an hour for
  something small, a day or two for an overhaul (PA5a, PA5b).
- **Speed:** add every bonus first, then multiply by the suit (PA8).
- **Open within his round-two answers:**
  - **VD2a.** He picked "the G7, the Striker XL and the Punknaught, plus any
    kitted vehicle", but his note says "vehicles that say 'armored' in their
    descriptions". Only the Striker XL's description uses the word.
  - **VD2b.** He picked "Massive damage", but his note says "Heavy and above",
    meaning the Heavy weapons such as grenade launchers.
- **Mod slots are out** (VK1, VK4, round one); what replaces them is
  round two's VK1a–VK4, **open**.

## Round one

### Powered Armor

| Q | Question | His pick | His note |
|---|---|---|---|
| PA1 | Is Powered Armor worn, or a vehicle? | Both. It's worn, and it uses the vehicle damage rules. Reword the book's sentence to say so. |  |
| PA2 | When someone in powered armor is hit, what stops the damage? | The suit takes it all: PROT, then RES, then Integrity. Nothing reaches the wearer until the suit is at 0 Integrity. |  |
| PA3 | Can you wear body armor under powered armor? | Under any suit. The vest counts after the suit. | “There is still a caveat here. Armors made of soft materials (Kevlar) or like clothing are wearable. Bulky armored suits and things like that won't work in a powered armor suit, there's no room for it.” |
| PA4 | What does a suit's RES work against? | Kinetic only, as a vehicle's RES. | “So, again there is a bit of a caveat here. I believe we'd been using vehicle armor as Integrity that gets depleted over time. We need to discuss this one because I am not sure that I want to have vehicles operate the exact same as worn armor.” |
| PA5 | When are a suit's enhancements chosen? | Both swap between scenes. | “I am not sure if I understand what you're asking with this one. Software is pretty much plug and play, no delays. Just like installing an application on your computer. Hardware changes are going to require skill checks and some time to implement. I just picked an answer to satisfy completion. We'll need to be sure this makes sense when you're ready.” |
| PA6 | “Chipped Skill: +3 to Martial Arts”: what kind of +3? | +3 ranks while the suit runs. An untrained skill rolls as trained at rank 3, without its Synergy. |  |
| PA7 | Which skills are “BOD based”? | BOD as Primary only: Athletics and Survival. |  |
| PA8 | How does Enhanced Mobility meet speed powers? | It multiplies after the power: they stack. | “This takes your baseline speeds and doubles them.” |
| PA9 | Are the suits' prices right? | Some are wrong. The right ones are in the note. | “Power Sleeves is pretty close. The other prices need to be increased. I would say a price increase of 70% pretty much. Maybe even 300% depending on the suit.” |

### A vehicle taking damage

| Q | Question | His pick | His note |
|---|---|---|---|
| VD1 | What order does a hit on a vehicle go in? | Not quite (say in the note). | “Again, needs discussing. I figure it is easier to deplete the vehicle Integrity as they take hits instead of having the PROT and RES involved. Vehicles will have a lot of Integrity because of what they are. It's something we should go over in person.” |
| VD2 | What does an ordinary weapon do to a vehicle? | Half damage, after PROT and RES. | “This is the closest answer I can see being right. It's really dependent on the vehicle. Does it have any armor? Yes? Then small arms fire, rifles, shotguns, and beam weapons are going to pretty much 0 damage. You need heavy weapons or explosives. No armor? Half seems right.” |
| VD3 | What percentage does an Integrity Check roll against? | Something else (say in the note). | “This goes into the system failure conversation we've had. At 30% integrity you're rolling a System Failure check to see if the vehicle loses it's ability to function.” |
| VD4 | “25% or more in a single round”: 25% of what? | Of its maximum Integrity. | “This may need to be higher. 40% or 50% perhaps.” |
| VD5 | How much does EMP Shielding help a vehicle? | Something else (say in the note). | “We should use the EM-Sheath concept here. 1-3 times that EMP can be ignored based on the quality of the vehicle.” |
| VD6 | How is a vehicle repaired? | The kit works on suits only. Vehicles need a garage. | “1d6 wouldn't be enough to be meaningful. You want someone who can get into the machine with a good Engineering skill to really repair the thing. Especially if you're banged up below 20% Integrity.” |
