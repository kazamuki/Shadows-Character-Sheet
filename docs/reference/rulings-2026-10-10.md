(*Rulings for Deighton*, a claude.ai artifact Ken shared with him) and
answered the evening of 2026-10-10. Ken answered the follow-ups the same
night, partly from Deighton and partly as his own calls; those are marked
**Ken**. Kept here word for word, because the page is not part of the repo.

## Taking damage

**Q1 · F23. Which armor class do Electric and Burning damage fall in?**
Pick: *Energy (keep the stand-in).* Note: "This would fit under the Ablative umbrella."

**Q2 · F23. When does the Resistance upgrade's 50% apply, relative to the PROT roll and RES?**
Pick: *Halve first, then PROT and RES.*

**Q3 · F25. How does Natural Armor reduce a hit?**
Pick: *Mostly right (see my note).* Note: "Natural armor reduces incoming damage including ballistic damage and ignores Armor Piercing modifiers except where magic is concerned. I believe that kinetic damage refers to bullets, melee weapons (knives, clubs, etc.), and so forth. Massive Damage does skip Natural Armor also. I figure that my description and yours line up nearly 100% for this type of armor."
Follow-up (Ken): AP on a spell **does** pierce Natural Armor.

**Q4 · F36. Is damage past zero kept, or does healing count up from 0 HP?**
Pick: *Discarded: healing counts from 0 HP.* Note: "Count healing for 0. The damage past 0 is just a reference really to show how badly the body is damaged when death saves are needed. Having to heal negative damage back will absorb healing items dramatically. However, as a side note, GM's can use negative damage for more gritty gameplay as they choose."
Follow-up (Ken): if a GM runs it the gritty way, the sheet goes along with it.

**Q5 · F24. When Bleeding or Burning ticks on a Dying character at a Reset, what happens?**
Pick: *The WILL check as usual, plus a mark per source.*

**Q6 · MD. Are MD ratings the rule for Massive damage now?**
Pick: *Stay with 1 HL per 10 points.* Note: "To be clear, this should be MD = 10 Integrity damage counted against the armor itself. I[f] that penetrates to the person piloting or wearing the powered armor it will do 1 Health Level of damage. This damage stacks consecutively each MD is 10 Integrity damage and 1 HL damage."
Follow-up (Ken): a Massive hit that drops armor to 0 still takes the extra Health Level ("I think so").

**Q7 · Grenades. Do the Thunderclap and the Junk Bomb deal Massive damage?**
Pick: *They deal Massive.* Note: "Grenades/Explosives are massive damage weapons."

## Weapons and ammo

**Q8 · F26. Does a shotgun count as a rifle for the Scope and the Angel Mod?**
Pick: *No (keep).* Note: "Don't keep scope or Angel Rounds. The configuration for Angle Rounds needs a box magazine with a power cell in it to properly fire AND shotguns are a rifle but they don't use scopes."

**Q9 · F30. What does the Anti-Materiel tag do when it hits a person?**
Pick: *Nothing extra against people.* Note: "The weapons with this tag usually have AP and high damage. The Anti-Materiel in this case means that it's not stopped by vehicle armor hence the damage affecting vehicles as the descriptor."

**Q10 · F31. What does the Reach tag add?**
Pick: *It adds something (see my note).* Note: "This number should be the number of feet that the weapon can extend out from the attacker. This could be something like 5 or 6 feet for the Mono Whip is why that is significant. Of course some melee weapons would technically have reach so it may be prudent to either apply it where needed or just use it sparingly for weapons that are unique. Again, just like the monowhip."
Follow-up (Ken): convert to **meters**, consistent with the rest of the sheet. (Ken raised a metric/imperial switch as an idea: W100.)

**Q11 · F34. When you reload before a magazine is empty, are the rounds left in it kept?**
Pick: *Kept, with its count.*

## Gear that boosts you (W69)

**Q12. When worn gear raises a stat, what does the bonus reach?**
Pick: *Skill checks and damage only.*

**Q13. Do two worn pieces that boost the same thing stack?**
Pick: *They stack.*

**Q14. For a modifier on "energy weapons", do Electric and Burning weapons count as energy?**
Pick: *Both count as energy.*

**Q15. Can a worn bonus push a score past 10?**
Pick: *Yes, worn bonuses can pass 10.*

**Q16. Is Powered Armor worn gear with modifiers, or a vehicle with its own damage track?**
Pick: *A vehicle, with its own Integrity.*

## Holding a spell (W87)

**Q17. Can an Arcanist hold two Concentration spells at once?**
Pick: *Several, and the dice add up.* Note: "Each spell sustained through concentration sequesters a die. The Arcanist can concentrate on as many spells as they have ranks in in Evocation."
Follow-up (Ken): "the held spell has to succeed, then it takes **one die** while you hold it, so any other spells you cast you throw 1 less die. And yes, you can only hold up to your Evocation rank in spells at a time." (`0480` says dice equal to the spell's Threshold; this replaces it.)

**Q18. Does Charging a spell interact with holding one?**
Pick: *No interaction: the set-aside applies to charging too.*

**Q19. When the Arcanist goes down from damage, should the hold drop on its own?**
Pick: *No, the GM decides when they're unconscious.* Note: "They need to make a WILL Ess. Check to determine if they lose their concentration. It's not a binary outcome."
Follow-up (Ken): anything that would sensibly break concentration calls for the check (taking damage, a Shock, going down). **The check's TN and TH are still to be set.**

## Vampires, Werewolves and movement

**Q20 · F41. Is the Upyr's Built to Last a choice made at character creation?**
Pick: *Hold both, they just don't stack.*
Follow-up (Ken): he had read it wrong; correct it.

**Q21 · Day Rest. What does "+2 to that day's Thirst" mean for a Vampire who skips Day Rest?**
Pick: *Something else (see my note).* Note: "It means that skipping rest will show as 1/2 Rate of Use per day +2 more SFR lost for not resting. It doesn't rise."

**Q22 · Supernatural ceilings. How far past 10 can a Werewolf's or Vampire's stats go, and do Skills and Powers get the same headroom?**
Note: "There is no ceiling to this. If you need a soft cap as a point of reference it can stop at 50 but there is actual limit here."
**Open:** whether "is actual limit" means "is *no* actual limit", and whether Skills and Powers share the headroom (so IP can raise them past 10). Ken is asking.

**Q23 · Jump. How far can a character jump?**
Note: "Jump is calculated like this: Sprint / 4. This is rounded down. When taking Fleet of Foot you multiply the Sprint value you by 2 first then divide by 4 after to get the Jump value."

## Advancement and Milestones

**Q24. When an Advantage is bought with IP, does every rank cost the same?**
Pick: *Flat: the same price every rank.*

**Q25. Can Talented raise a rank of any Advantage the character has, or only one worth 5 points or less?**
Pick: *Any Advantage.* Note: "When buying a new Advantage it has to be 5 points or less. Owned Advantages can be upgraded with this Milestone without that prerequisite."

**Q26. Do the rebalanced Minor Milestone values stand?**
Pick: *Use the rebalanced values.* (Skilled: two Skills, one rank each. Improved: 3d10+30 IP. Redeemed: a Disadvantage worth 5 points or less.)

**Q27. Can a Minor Milestone be taken again before the others have all been taken once?**
Pick: *Any can repeat at any time.*

**Q28. Can Honed or Skilled push a score past 10?**
Pick: *Capped at 10 (keep).*

**Q29 · W39. How does a character learn a new Martial Arts style in play, and what do style bonuses like +1 Stun and +1 Grapple add to?**
Note: "There will need to be some role play for this. They can find a mentor to learn the first rank or some other means of learning (i.e. the LINK where they can train more quickly using neural-ware). But there should be an in-game catalyst for that. They can train afterward on their own or through their combat experience."
Follow-up (Ken): Stun and Grapple are attack Conditions, so a style makes you better at inflicting that Condition. IP is spent on the Martial Arts skill as a whole, which makes every Martial Arts check easier; a style isn't bought with IP.

**Q34 · GQ30. In Wave Initiative, does the side with the best Combat Sense result go first, then the next best?**
Pick: *Differently (see my note).* Note: "Based on the scenario, the player closest to danger/action is rolls their Combat Sense. The enemy rolls theirs. After this, during the players turn the high roll will start with their actions. The battle is decided organically after this because actions change the combat scenario. Players will discuss, move, attack, hold actions, or whatever they can during their wave. Once all actions are taken for the players side, it flips the enemy and they will attack in whatever way makes sense based on their numbers or abilities. The GM has a lot of leeway given the state of play but the waves will flip back and forth and who starts first is decided based on the best Combat Sense roll for that wave. If Combat Sense is needed again during that wave the best roll will lead the next wave when the turn resets."
Follow-up (Ken): whether the order is rolled again "depends. Sometimes things change in the fight that causes a re-ordering. Other times it stays the same throughout."
