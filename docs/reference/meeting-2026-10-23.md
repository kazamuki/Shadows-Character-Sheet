## Natural attacks: two a round, by default

**The problem** (Deighton, 91:54–101:33). The mastery Advantages are mortal-only,
so a Vampire or Werewolf had no way to make more than one attack a round. In
the last playtest, Nye's Vampire made one attack while everyone else made two
or three. Options weighed: open the masteries to every archetype, a
low-prerequisite Milestone, or a supernatural power.

**Where it landed** (Deighton, 117:11–118:48; Ken, 118:42–119:20):

- **Natural attacks are their own category, and a default.** They aren't an
  Advantage, and they don't belong to an archetype. No Advantage changes.
- **What counts:** natural weapons, such as claws and teeth. On the mortal side,
  fists. A carried weapon doesn't count ("If I'm carrying a knife, that's not a
  natural weapon"). Blades in a cyborg's fists or knuckle studs **add damage to
  the fists** and are still natural attacks.
- **Two a round,** in any mix: "claw-bite or claw-claw, but you get two." In the
  action economy, a character making natural attacks can make more than one,
  with no mastery needed.
- **It stops at two.** A third (Scott's "claw, claw, bite") may come later
  through Milestones (Ken, 119:20), along with moving between targets without
  an opportunity attack (Scott, 119:30). Neither is ruled yet.

**Against the book.** `0530`'s *Making an Attack* says "You get one Standard
Action per turn. Some Advantages, Milestones, or Powers may grant an
additional attack". The meeting adds a default on top of that, so the
chapter needs a line (`plans/crb-catch-up.md`, 0530).

**Still for Deighton** (it was never asked; `plans/loadout-and-gear.md` §6):
1. Are the two attacks two separate rolls, each against its own defence?
2. Do both fit inside the one Standard Action, or does the second cost
   something?
3. Can a Martial Arts Combat Maneuver (Stun, Disarm) be one of the two?
4. Can one natural attack and one carried weapon share a round, such as a knife
   and a bite?
5. Do two natural attacks need two free limbs, so a character holding a
   two-handed weapon has no claws free?

**What it changes on the sheet:** `plans/loadout-and-gear.md` L4. Nothing is
built yet.
