## The seven questions: unanimous

All three picked the same answer on every question. These feed P19 and P20.

- **Sanity cuts** (Blood Lust, Berserker, Fanatic, Hallucinations, Minor Insanity): *Apply it: points off the max (5% is 5 points).* Scott: "So long as this means your reducing max Sanity when taking the DIS, this is correct."
- **Pain Levels** (Terminal Disease, Pain Tolerance): *Apply both: Terminal Disease adds, then Pain Tolerance takes off.*
- **Stat maximums** (Age, Dwarf, Giant): *Cap them in the wizard; Giant's BOD 11 lifts the cap.* Ken: "I could see someone saying that a GM could override this, but that could be after creation, I think?"
- **A Skill's check** (Ghost Step, Defect/Flaw, Field Medic, Hyper Vigilance): *Put them all on the Skill rows.* Ken: "Does kicker dice stack? if they do, does it move to the next die size up?" **Open:** nobody answered it, and the core rules don't say (only Field Medic's own text, "can stack with other kicker die").
- **Starting Çredits** (Wealthy): *Ç10,000, once.* Ken: "Each rank grants 2000 credits per month as well." Deighton: "Each rank adds 2000 credits per month as well. That said, I would like to revisit this simply because we don't actually track "months" and it would be cool to maybe apply these things to Milestones. Like every Minor/Major milestone, you also get a payday of either UBI or your wealthy advantage or something."
- **Healing** (Rapid Healing): *Multiply natural healing; it stacks with SpeedHeal.*
- **Take a hit** (Scrapper, Giant, Thick Skull): *Apply all three in Take a hit.* Deighton: "What is "hand to hand damage" ? I feel like this should be simplified to melee and martial arts, like Giant."

**Ken's calls on the two questions:**
- **Melee Master counts Martial Arts** ("in context, it would make sense that a melee attack would count martial arts"). **Thrown weapons: open.** Ken is asking the group.

## Deighton's notes for a design talk

Proposals and questions on entries he signed as drafted. None is built or
changes P18's text. They're **W130**'s list.

- **Age:** "I feel like MOB should not be part of this. kids are freaking fast. Also how does this apply with like Werewolves and Vampires and stuff?" *(Bears on P20's Age cap.)*
- **Favored Skill:** "I think we need something like "and take the higher result""
- **Nine Lives:** "Maybe you can spend one of the lives to automatically succeed a Death Save"
- **Snatch:** "or deflect it? I don't want to catch a bullet!"
- **True Faith:** "I have never seen an encounter last 12 rounds. That seems like overkill."
- **Unshakeable:** "I don't know what this means. Surprise only grants +1 to the attack roll." (He quoted the book's Surprise rules: +1 to the first attack; Fast Actions only on the first turn; no penalty to defense.)
- **Medium:** "Needs a mechanical benefit. You can x with spirits - speak, see, hear, etc. since we have Spirit as a Supernatural Threat type there has to be a mechanical benefit here, not just a narrative benefit."
- **Favors:** "This is another 'roleplay' type advantage that has no mechanical benefit. I feel like the favor should be from a named person, so it's not just an open ended thing. and also GM discretion, like the Contacts."
- **Fanatic:** "This is a disadvantage that will likely never actually hit. Maybe MAG Skill Checks have a -1 penalty?"
- **Gullible:** "If we're doing contested checks for lying, that would be likely a contested Deception. In that case, we could say "Contested Deception checks to spot a lie are at a -2 penalty" or whatever language is equivalent."
- **Hemophiliac:** "The Bleeding condition causes -1 damage per round. It is resolved by making a BOD Essence TN 8 TH 2, using a Nanomed kit, or someone making a "Stop the Bleeding" action on you (Medical 15). This could be updated to say "Bleeding causes you to lose 2 health per turn, and requires a BOD Essence Check TN 8 TH 3, or Medical check at difficulty 18.""
- **Ghost TAG(s):** "What is "corporate AI" in this sense?"
- **Dream Walker:** "I don't know how this is intended to actually work. a photograph of a person lets you enter their dream anywhere in the world?"
- **Followers/Minion:** "I am ok with this but we'll need to define what a minion or companion is. Are they controlled by GM or player? are they a combatant? What are their stats/skills?" *(W123.)*
- **Poverty:** "I still don't know how this would get enforced. but I agree a tracker should work. I also think, like wealthy, you can pay this off each Milestone until it disappears on its own. Like getting garnished, essentially. I also want this to be to a specific person or corporation." *(W124.)*
- **Wealthy:** "I'd love if wealthy paid out at each milestone." *(W129.)*
- **Reputation** (to discuss): "I have a proposal for this related to CRANK - Reputation increases your CRANK reputation, allowing higher earnings." *(The CRANK talk; CRANK is switched off, Decision 214.)*
- **Addiction** (to discuss): "there are a lot of things that relate to Addiction mechanic so it will be good to discuss in general." *(W125.)*
- **Thick Skin:** "AP only bypasses RES, but still hits PROT and still hits Natural Armor." *(Agrees with the book's "Natural Armor is unaffected by Armor Piercing" and Decision 219's exception for spells; P18's tag says so.)*
